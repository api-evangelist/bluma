# Bluma - Automated Video Content Creation Platform

## About Bluma
Bluma is an AI-powered video generation platform that helps businesses create UGC (User-Generated Content) and faceless videos at scale. We clone viral video formats into daily content that can be distributed across multiple social media accounts. Bluma helps founders and marketers automate daily UGC and faceless video creation to post across multiple accounts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Bluma?**
We clone viral formats into daily UGC and faceless videos that you can distribute across multiple accounts.

**What is a template?**
A reusable video structure modeled on a viral video where your content slots into a proven framework.

**How is it different from a regular video?**
Templates automate the repetitive work—one structure generates unlimited unique videos with different inputs.

**What differentiates Bluma from other AI tools?**
Traditional tools make 1-to-1 videos while Bluma makes 1-to-N, scaling one template into daily videos on autopilot.

**Do templates ever go stale?**
Trends change, but our tech allows us to constantly make new templates to stay on top of new formats.

**Can I customize the videos?**
Business users choose from our template library while Enterprise gets fully custom templates cloned from any viral videos.

**What types of assets do you use?**
A mix of AI-generated and real clips including images, voices, product footage, stock clips, and screenshots—whatever fits your brand.

**Do I need video editing experience?**
No. Business users get ready-to-post videos while Enterprise users get automatic posting across all their accounts.
